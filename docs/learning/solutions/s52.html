Worked answers

  1. It supplies topic descriptions and timestamps, not full methods, assumptions, derivations, or results. Detailed claims require the recording, transcript, or underlying paper. The offline companion therefore marks its toy mathematics as original teaching material.
  2. Model 1 retains $Y=X=0$. Model 2 uses intervened $H=1$, so $Y=1$. Both directions of intervention distinguish the causal structures despite identical natural observations.
  3. Ask whether a proposed motif predicts the result of interchange interventions across held-out inputs and models. Record the motif's variables, alignment to network states, counterfactual predictions, and failures. This would test an explanatory claim; merely spotting an attention pattern or citing a timestamp would not.
