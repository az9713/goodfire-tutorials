Features as Rewards: Using Interpretability to Reduce Hallucinations

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. Transfer indicates the learned signal remained predictive on evaluated trained-policy activations. A future policy can still generate novel probe-blind outputs, and even a stable representation can encode a biased reward. Test transfer, calibration, and behavioral validity separately under stronger optimization.
  2. The repair fixes 80 of the 160 detected errors, while false-positive corruption still adds four. Residual errors are $200-80+4=124$. Relative reduction is $(200-124)/200=0.38$, or 38%.
  3. Evaluate matched prompts with independent claim labels, count correct informative claims, score coverage of requested facts, and measure unwarranted abstention. Compare responses at matched length or information coverage. An error-rate decrease paired with a collapse in correct factual content is a different outcome from improved factual recall.
