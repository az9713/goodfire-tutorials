Worked solutions · S10

Forking Fast: Efficiently Estimating Uncertainty Dynamics in Text Generation

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked answers

  1. Flat regions may dominate the number of positions. Their variance reduction can outweigh the bias introduced around a few sharp forks. Report fork-local error and change-point localization alongside pooled TVD.
  2. Raw variance is $0.6(0.4)/20=0.012$ and standard error $0.10954$. Pooling five independent positions gives variance $0.0024$ and standard error $0.04899$. The gain depends on stationarity and independence; neither is guaranteed along a real base path.
  3. Collect independent continuation pools A and B per position. Fit and tune the low-budget estimator using only A with internal folds, and evaluate against B. Repeat with roles exchanged or use leave-replicate-out references. Retain a reference-noise estimate and report token costs. Shared samples mechanically shrink measured discrepancy, so nested estimates require explicit correction or clearly limited descriptive use.
