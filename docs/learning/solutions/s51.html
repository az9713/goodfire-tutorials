Worked solutions · S51

Interpretability Infrastructure at Frontier Scale: Harvesting Activations from a Trillion-Parameter Model

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked answers

  1. Rows can be assigned to wrong sequences or offsets, duplicated across ranks, or overwritten before writing. Shape checks detect none of these. Scientific interpretation requires a verified relation between vector and original context.
  2. Time is $10^9/20000=50000$ seconds, about 13.89 hours. Storage is $10^9(4096)(2)=8.192\times10^{12}$ bytes, or 8.192 decimal TB before compression and metadata.
  3. Create unique short logical sequences with known token IDs and split them at awkward boundaries. Schedule them concurrently in varied orders. Compare stored vectors and absolute offsets against an unbatched reference, assert exactly-once coverage, and test buffer reuse under delayed writes. Include identical prefixes so cache reuse cannot silently erase sequence-specific provenance.
