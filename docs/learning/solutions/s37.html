  1. Matching outputs constrains sampled points but not how their internal representations relate. Lookup features can match each case while discarding the continuous or compositional structure joining cases.
  2. The derivative is $2h=2$, so the linear prediction is 2. Exact change is $(1+1)^2-1=3$. The missing quadratic term is 1; attribution becomes inaccurate for this finite edit.
  3. Hide a known mechanism or planted model behavior, allow tools a fixed compute budget, and require advance predictions for held-out counterfactual interventions and failure cases. Score predictive accuracy and discovery cost against behavioral-only and probe baselines. Labels support communication, while held-out predictions supply the verifiable target.
