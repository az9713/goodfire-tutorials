Worked solutions · S46

Goodfire and Baseten partner to bring frontier safety to open models

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked answers

  1. Categories have different data, labels, distributions, and failure costs. A shared serving interface does not make their detectors statistically identical. The announcement reports no common cross-category operating point.
  2. Require $b\geq0.9/(0.95\cdot0.99)\approx0.95694$, assuming these are the relevant conditional probabilities. The gate must block at least about 95.69% of delivered detected harmful actions.
  3. Use harmless simulated tools and predefined flags. Verify each configured action—logging, review, refusal, rerouting—under concurrency, timeout, retry, and cancellation. Record whether execution precedes review. Keep classifier-quality tests separate from routing and enforcement tests so a passing interface test cannot conceal missed detections.
