Predictive Data Debugging: Reveal and Shape What Your Model Learns, Before You Train

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. The selected examples may be one of several independent sources of fabricated-link learning; alternatively, the correct source cluster may be identified but the edit may be too weak or remove only a correlated aspect. A challenging explanation is that the feature primarily reads answer length or sensitive-topic language, so its apparent URL relevance disappears after matching those factors. Test this using matched examples and new prompts, then compare retraining outcomes.
  2. At zero margin the sigmoid factor remains $1/2$. Thus $\Delta\theta=0.2(2)(1/2)(4/3,1/3)=(4/15,1/15)$. Later margins enter $\sigma(-\beta r_i)$, gradient directions and representations change, and the output probability is nonlinear in the parameters. Only the first update has doubled under these assumptions.
  3. Hold the optimizer steps and training-token budget fixed, resampling retained data if appropriate. Compare targeted filtering with random filtering, reduced-learning-rate training, and balanced rewrites. Measure intended-task gain and failure reduction jointly. If targeted removal performs no better than random removal at matched capability gain, there is little evidence for selective mitigation; if it transfers across held-out contexts while random removal does not, selectivity is better supported.
