Worked solutions · S54

On Optimism for Interpretability

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. The number of states and interventions can be enormous, several explanations can fit observed data, and the actual mechanism may require complex interactions. Access permits experiments but does not solve search, identifiability, or explanatory compression.
  2. Total time becomes $0.8/5+0.2=0.36$, giving speedup $1/0.36\approx2.78$. Infinite labeling speed leaves 0.2, so the limit is fivefold if the remaining work is unchanged.
  3. Hide known mechanisms in small models, require agents to preregister counterfactual predictions, and score held-out intervention outcomes. Include unrelated controls and penalties for unsupported claims. Use new model seeds and tasks after development so success cannot come from memorized feature names or benchmark-specific narratives.
