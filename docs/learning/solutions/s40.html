Worked solutions · S40

Painting with concepts using diffusion model latents

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. The global feature is a fixed dictionary direction learned across training images. A factor is a blend fitted to one image's activations and spatial arrangement. Its meaning and index need not transfer to another image, and it may combine multiple global features.
  2. Each first-factor contribution becomes $(2S_{p1})(B_{1k}/2)=S_{p1}B_{1k}$ for every patch and feature. The second-factor term is untouched, so every entry of $SB$ stays fixed.
  3. Use many held-out scenes and seeds, predefined source/target positions, and independent assessments of identity, location, background changes, and other objects. Compare latent movement with prompt-only edits at matched generation budget. Failures in identity or collateral edits must remain visible rather than selecting only successful examples.
