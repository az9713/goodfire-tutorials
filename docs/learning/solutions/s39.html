Replicating Circuit Tracing for a Simple Known Mechanism

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. Parity concerns evenness, not order. A parity signal can correlate with plausible continuations or serve another context, while producing invalid year preferences for this task.
  2. The summed write is $2[(1,0)+(0,3)]=(2,6)$. Dotting with $(4,-1)$ gives $8-6=2$. This is a direct readout contribution, not necessarily the total causal effect of ablating the feature.
  3. Discover across multiple inputs, group features by shared read/write structure, and test coordinated interventions that shift a proposed numerical variable. Evaluate held-out input ranges and compare global geometric models with lookup models. Reconstruction quality and one-prompt heatmaps alone cannot decide the issue.
