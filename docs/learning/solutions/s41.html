Under the Hood of a Reasoning Model

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. It may detect language associated with backtracking rather than incorrect reasoning or the operation that repairs it. Matched phrases, error labels, and causal effects on successful correction are needed to distinguish those possibilities.
  2. The derivative is positive before one and negative afterward because $e^{-s}$ is positive. The second derivative at one is $-e^{-1}\lt0$. Exponential decay dominates the linear factor as $s$ grows, so the limit is zero.
  3. Identify a proposed repair mediator independently, then ablate it during the strength sweep while checking that baseline task performance remains interpretable. If the return persists unchanged despite effective removal, that mediator is not necessary for the phenomenon. A failure to identify any mediator leaves the repair story unconfirmed rather than disproved.
