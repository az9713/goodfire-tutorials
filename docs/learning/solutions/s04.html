Worked solutions

  1. A probe measures an association between hidden state and label. In the parabola example, $h_1$ decodes position perfectly, but a decoder also checks $h_2=h_1^2$. Changing one coordinate without maintaining the relation violates a condition invisible to the probe.
  2. For $0\leq a\leq1$, the endpoints are $(-a,a^2)$ and $(a,a^2)$. Their chord midpoint is $(0,a^2)$; the manifold midpoint is $g(0)=(0,0)$. Euclidean discrepancy is $a^2$, and squared discrepancy is $a^4$. The quadratic dependence is the curvature effect.
  3. Collect held-out states spanning position and momentum. Fit the candidate position curve while conditioning on momentum, then compare manifold edits, matched-size line edits, and natural-state swaps. Evaluate decoded position, independent momentum estimates, next-frame consistency under several actions, and perceptual quality. A successful position edit that corrupts momentum is partial control rather than a clean intervention on one variable.
