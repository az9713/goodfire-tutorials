Intentionally designing the future of AI

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. The frozen evaluator maps the student's tokens to fixed reference activations, so rotating the student's own activations does not directly change its reward. The policy can still produce token sequences that exploit the evaluator's learned correlations, such as omitting hard claims or using misleading formulations. Validate rewards with independent factual or behavioral judgments.
  2. No pirate change requires $\delta\theta_2=0$. Then the norm bound gives $\delta\theta_1\le0.15$, so the maximum math increase is $0.15$. The ordinary update gives $0.2$ math and $0.1$ pirate change with norm about $0.1414$. Selectivity costs attainable math gain under this particular budget and representation.
  3. Train with a cheap frozen probe, but verify on held-out prompt families using independently collected behavioral labels, causal interventions, and an expressive interpretation method with a different representation assumption. Keep verification labels and adversarial search outcomes out of training selection. Evaluate newly induced contexts and repeat after further optimization. This reduces shared failure channels but cannot establish independence merely by naming different tools; measure correlated errors explicitly.
