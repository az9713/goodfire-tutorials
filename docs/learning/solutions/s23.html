Covariance-based Sequence Pooling

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked answers

  1. No. Row permutation $P$ gives $P^\top P=I$, hence the second moment is unchanged. Order may already influence contextual embeddings, but identical rows in a different order yield identical pooled output.
  2. Subtract $\mu\mu^\top=\frac14\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$. The results are $\frac14\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$ and $\frac14\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}$, respectively. Centering preserves this particular distinction.
  3. Use fixed train/test gene splits and equal tuning budgets. Compare a linear mean probe, a capacity-matched nonlinear mean probe, fixed-projection second moments, and learned second moments. Add synthetic tasks with equal means and controlled co-occurrence. Improvements on the controlled task isolate available information; genomic generalization still requires independent held-out testing.
