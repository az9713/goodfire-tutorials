Worked solutions · S27

Using Interpretability to Identify a Novel Class of Alzheimer's Biomarkers

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked answers

  1. The proxy tests whether explicit measurements retain predictive information outside the complex model. Confounding can make those measurements predictive without placing them on a causal disease pathway. Model simplification and causal identification are different achievements.
  2. The design effect is $1+99(0.2)=20.8$. The effective count for the mean is $8000/20.8\approx384.62$. This approximation does not create 385 independently labeled patients; there remain 80 patient units for patient-level disease inference.
  3. Recruit a larger prospective cohort across sites, randomize processing batches across case status, standardize handling, blind laboratories, and record relevant covariates. Freeze feature definitions and coefficients before external testing. Include disease controls and replicate assays. Test predictive transport separately from mechanistic experiments; even excellent external accuracy would not alone identify the biological cause.
