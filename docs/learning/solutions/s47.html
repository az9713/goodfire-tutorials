Worked solutions · S47

We can and must solve alignment

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. Check the feature against independently labeled honesty across contexts, intervene while controlling correlated style and uncertainty, and test incentives and distribution shifts. Establish that effects persist under optimization and compare mechanisms supporting truthful behavior. Even these results would have a defined scope rather than prove an unrestricted moral property.
  2. Require $(1-0.001)^n\le0.05$, so $n\ge\log(0.05)/\log(0.999)\approx2994.23$. The smallest integer is 2,995. Dependence or distribution shift invalidates the simple binomial interpretation.
  3. Define a hidden-shortcut task with held-out contexts. Give one team internal tools and another stronger behavioral tools with matched compute, labels, and time. Measure discovery, mitigation, collateral capability loss, and transfer to unseen contexts. A consistent advantage linked to correctly identified mechanisms supports practical bottleneck relief; no advantage would challenge the claim for that task.
