Worked solutions · S18

Steering Along Manifolds to Control Neural Networks

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. The naturally occupied set can be nonconvex. Convex combinations need not stay inside it; the decoder may be unconstrained away from observed states.
  2. Rotate the pair to angles $\pm\Delta/2$. Their midpoint is $(\cos(\Delta/2),0)$ with radius $\cos(\Delta/2)$. The distance to the circle is $1-\cos(\Delta/2)$, approximately $\Delta^2/8$ for small $\Delta$.
  3. Hold out templates and day-offset combinations, compare edits at matched behavior distance and activation displacement, measure off-weekday probability and unrelated-answer preservation, and include natural activation swaps. Evaluate in the full distribution before using a low-dimensional plot.
