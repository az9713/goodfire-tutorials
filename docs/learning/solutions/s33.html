Understanding sparse autoencoder scaling in the presence of feature manifolds

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. Extra latents can reduce approximation or sparsity cost on an already represented manifold. The optimization objective rewards loss reduction, not semantic novelty.
  2. The ratio is $(0.36/0.04)^{1/2}=3$. Integer allocation and discovery thresholds can modify this continuous optimum.
  3. Mix a known common curved factor with many independent rare discrete factors in orthogonal subspaces. Sweep width and sparsity, then match each learned latent to ground truth, count discovered factors, and measure tiling density separately. Vary radial noise and overlap to test sensitivity to the model's assumptions.
