Worked solutions

  1. It prevents simultaneous deletion of information the SAE did not reconstruct. The decoder direction may still encode multiple properties or interact nonlinearly downstream, so the intended concept is not isolated by the algebra alone.
  2. The modified score is $(z_a+c)-V^{-1}\sum_j(z_j+c)=G$. In softmax, the factor $e^c$ cancels between numerator and denominator. A common-mode logit gradient is therefore irrelevant to token choice.
  3. On fixed prompts and token positions, estimate $[G(h+\delta e_k)-G(h-\delta e_k)]/(2\delta)$ for decreasing positive $\delta$, where $e_k$ changes one feature. Compare with automatic differentiation, preserving residuals. Disagreement at small scales suggests implementation or nondifferentiability issues; disagreement only at large scales indicates curvature and limits of the local approximation.
