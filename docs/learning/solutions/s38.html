Worked solutions · S38

Towards Scalable Parameter Decomposition

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked answers

  1. The dataset may not activate a component or contain the counterfactual that reveals its role. Minimality is relative to input distribution, fidelity tolerance, architecture, and admissible interventions. Global claims require stronger coverage or formal assumptions.
  2. The mean removed vector is $(1,1.5)$ with squared norm $3.25$. The variance term is $0.25(4+9)=3.25$. Total expected distortion is $6.5$.
  3. Measure single, joint, and randomized ablations across held-out inputs, including inputs chosen to activate proposed mechanisms independently. Compare the sum of single-component effects with joint effects. Large cancellation or synergy indicates that individual labels or importance scores are insufficient. Preserve an intact-model reference and report off-distribution behavior of masked networks.
