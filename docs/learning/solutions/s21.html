Worked solutions

  1. It holds the activation coordinate system fixed, so differences primarily reflect different processed responses. Directly subtracting independently changed internal representations can confound response content with representation drift, even when dimensions agree.
  2. Positive scaling cancels between cosine numerator and norm, so $s_1$ stays about 0.8944. The dot product rises from 4 to 40. A dot-product ranking can therefore be dominated by magnitude while cosine emphasizes orientation.
  3. Estimate length contrasts independently and create a length-only ranking. Compare it with the behavior ranking, random filtering, and behavior ranking within narrow length bins, all at matched edit count and compute. Evaluate harmful compliance, benign instruction following, verbosity, and overrefusal separately. If length-only filtering explains the entire gain, the claim of a specific compliance signal is weakened.
