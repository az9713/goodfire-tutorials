Worked answers

  1. Its output vocabulary and supervision specify the annotation categories. A new mechanism outside that vocabulary may affect embeddings but has no dedicated output. Unsupervised exploration can suggest additional hypotheses, but still needs biological validation.
  2. $\sigma(0)=1/2$ and $\sigma(-\log3)=1/(1+3)=1/4$, so the change is $-1/4$. It is a 25-percentage-point decrease in the probe's predicted annotation probability, not a measured 25% decrease in function.
  3. Assign entire genes and overlapping regulatory neighborhoods to one split, fit preprocessing only on training genes, and reserve a later temporal cohort as an additional test. Report consequence-specific discrimination, calibration, and label uncertainty. Independently validate a preregistered subset of mechanisms with appropriate assays. Gene holdout tests a stronger claim than random variant splitting, while still allowing evolutionary or annotation correlations.
