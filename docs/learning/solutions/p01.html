Linear algebra for reading neural computations

Background for the Goodfire essays. Read any chapter when useful; no placement test or progress gate is required.

Solutions

  1. $W_2W_1=(3,5)$. At $(2,1)$ the output is $3\cdot2+5\cdot1=11$. Computing the hidden vector first gives $(4,1)$ and then $3\cdot4-1=11$, an independent arithmetic check.

  2. The variance fraction is $100/101$. Independence makes the covariance between $z$ and $t$ zero, and in the stipulated independent construction $z$ carries no information about $t$. Retaining almost all variance can discard all target information.

  3. Record activations at a chosen linear interface, apply an invertible $A$, and replace the downstream readout by $WA^{-1}$. Verify identical outputs over a fixed test set. Compare coordinate-label associations before and after, while transforming any direction-based probe consistently. Do not move $A$ across a nonlinearity unless an explicit calculation proves equivalence.
