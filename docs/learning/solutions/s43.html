Worked solutions · S43

Open Problems in Mechanistic Interpretability

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. A probe can monitor a correlated warning signal if held-out performance remains useful. Claiming that the probed direction implements or mediates the behavior requires intervention or another causal identification strategy.
  2. $N\geq\log(0.05)/\log(0.99)\approx298.07$, so at least 299 independent cases are required. This is not a guarantee against an unknown or adversarial failure distribution.
  3. Plant a behavior in a model with a controlled mechanism, hide that mechanism from investigators, and ask for held-out intervention predictions plus a targeted repair. Compare circuit tools against behavioral search and ordinary fine-tuning at matched cost. Score prediction, repair specificity, and unintended degradation separately. Repeat across different planted mechanisms to reduce benchmark overfitting.
