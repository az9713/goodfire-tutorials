Worked solutions · S50

AI Safety Still Needs Great Engineers

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked answers

  1. Prioritizing urgent marginal impact over retraining or personal research interests is a value judgment, informed by uncertain forecasts. The essay offers reasons and examples, not a universal empirical ranking of career paths.
  2. Initially $H=500(1-0.4)=300$. At coverage $0.75$, $H=500(1-0.6)=200$, a reduction of 100 under the stated homogeneous assumptions.
  3. Measure detector effectiveness on representative events, then audit what fraction of real events receive timely, functioning protection. Attribute uncovered events to integration, cost, compatibility, or policy barriers. Compare feasible gains from coverage improvements with gains from a better detector, including costs and uncertainty. Avoid treating an offline score as evidence of live coverage.
