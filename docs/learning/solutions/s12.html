Worked solutions · S12

Meandering on Manifolds: The Neural Geometry of Stories Over Time

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. The rating prompt changes the task and its activations may encode instructions or answer preparation. Collecting activations without it asks whether the information is present during ordinary reading. It still requires controls for correlated textual cues.
  2. Prior odds are $(1/3)/(2/3)=1/2$. After the first sentence odds are $3$, so probability is $3/4$. After the second odds are $1$, so probability is $1/2$.
  3. Write matched stories with identical sentiment words but reversed causal outcomes, and paraphrase each using different vocabulary. Split by underlying plot and template. Compare unprompted activation probes with a bag-of-words baseline and behavioral ratings. Better plot generalization supports a richer interpretation; causal steering would provide a separate test.
