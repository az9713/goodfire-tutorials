Can SAEs Capture Neural Geometry?

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. A decoder reconstructs combinations of atoms. A continuous factor may alter several coefficients jointly, and local tiles may use different atoms. Reconstruction tests their sum, not the semantic completeness of any individual atom.
  2. The worst discrepancy is $\pi/6$, so error is $2-2\cos(\pi/6)=2-\sqrt3\approx0.268$. This is squared distance, not distance.
  3. Hold vocabulary and syntax approximately fixed while varying temperature, then paraphrase across unrelated domains such as cooking, materials, and weather. Test ordered geometric position on unseen contexts and steer it while monitoring temperature-specific outputs and unrelated attributes. A cluster that follows only weather words fails the abstraction test.
