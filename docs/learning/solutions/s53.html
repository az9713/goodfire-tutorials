You and Your Research Agent: Lessons From Using Agents for Interpretability Research

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked answers

  1. Tests can establish that code implements its specification while the specification uses invalid labels, confounded data, an inappropriate estimator, or a misleading comparison. Scientific validity concerns the inference from observations to a claim, beyond software execution.
  2. The probability is $1-0.99^{10}\approx0.09562$. Bonferroni uses $0.05/10=0.005$ per test, controlling familywise error by the union bound without requiring independence. Exploratory selection still requires honest reporting and preferably fresh confirmation.
  3. Give the critic raw outputs, data identifiers, code, and a preregistered claim rather than only the final narrative. Require an independent recomputation of the main statistic, a negative control, a leakage audit, and a clean-state execution check. Record disagreements and unresolved assumptions. A critic's approval is evidence of review, not a theorem of correctness.
