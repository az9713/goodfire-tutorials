Logits as a new monitor for evaluation awareness — LessWrong

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked answers

  1. The score refers precisely to one observable token sequence. Awareness is a broader latent construct. Other phrasings, style preferences, and suppression of verbalization break any direct equality between them.
  2. The product is $0.004$. At-least-once probability is $1-0.996^{100}\approx0.3302$. Thus even 100 draws often show no exact phrase despite a measurable graded score.
  3. Create evaluation and deployment-like prompts with identical system scaffolds, lengths, and formatting. Randomize task presentation within matched semantic pairs, freeze monitored phrases on a separate development set, and hold out task families. Compare score distributions and AUROC before and after matching; a large drop implicates formatting as part of the original signal.
