Uncovering Neural Geometry in Vision Models With Block-Sparse Featurizers

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. Orthogonal rotations of internal axes preserve the block's reconstruction and norm. The region occupied by arch activations can be coherent even when no particular basis vector corresponds to a named part.
  2. The covariance eigenvalues are $2,2,1/2,1/2$. Their sum is $5$ and squared sum is $8.5$, giving $25/8.5=50/17$. The intrinsic dimension remains one.
  3. Generate held-out mixtures of known curved factors, match total active scalar coordinates and measured description length, and compare reconstruction, factor grouping, and intervention paths. Sweep both methods' capacities. In a real model add held-out examples and controls for image quality; synthetic recovery and real-model causality answer different questions.
