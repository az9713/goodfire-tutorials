Worked solutions · S20

Verbalized Eval Awareness Inflates Measured Safety

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked answers

  1. The sentence may mention policy, danger, intent, or social context. Deletion changes all its information and the continuation prefix. The identified effect belongs to the specified deletion/resampling intervention, not an isolated latent mental property.
  2. The probabilities are $1-0.99^{100}\approx0.6340$ and $1-0.99^{500}\approx0.9934$. The same underlying prompt can therefore move between “ever verbalizes” groups solely because of the sampling budget.
  3. Have independent blinded reviewers rate preservation of the requested action, stakes, authorization, and specificity. Compare targeted rewrites with equally sized neutral edits, using held-out prompts and fresh rollouts. Measure refusal, awareness, and task interpretation separately. A reduction that survives semantic matching supports the realism hypothesis more strongly; it still does not establish deployment-wide safety.
