Feature Steering for Reliable and Expressive AI Engineering

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. Steering modifies a forward-pass state and typically must be applied again during inference. Fine-tuning changes persistent model parameters through optimization. Both can affect outputs, but their costs, transfer behavior, knowledge effects, and failure modes differ.
  2. Detail change is $-0.1(0.5)+0.3/6=0$. Length change is $-20(0.5)+10/6=-25/3$, about -8.33 tokens. This smaller operating point preserves detail under the toy linear assumptions.
  3. Predefine target lengths and factual/detail constraints, then test held-out prompt families with repeated sampling. Compare calibrated prompts and steering at matched tuning budget, report deviation from target, constraint violations, coherence, and cost. Check transfer after a model update; a smooth slider on a few examples does not establish reliable control.
