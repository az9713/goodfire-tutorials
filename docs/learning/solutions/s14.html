Worked solutions · S14

Why Larger Models Learn More: Effects of Capacity, Interference, and Rare-Task Retention

These answers are available from the start. Work the exercises before reading them if you want an independent self-check.

Worked solutions

  1. The model may represent a task alone but allocate its shared dimensions to higher-utility tasks under the weighted objective. This is a training-distribution and resource-allocation limitation.
  2. Let rare frequency be $p$. Compare $p$ with $0.2(1-p)$. The rare feature wins when $1.2p\gt0.2$, or $p\gt1/6$. At equality the optimum has a tie and need not select one uniquely.
  3. With batch size $B$, rare fraction $p$, and gap $G$, inject $GBp$ rare examples per cycle while adjusting other batches to keep total examples and frequencies matched. Track immediate gain after injection and decay before the next injection, alongside common-task performance. Control optimizer state and batch-size effects where possible.
