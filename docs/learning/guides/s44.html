A feature map is an exploration tool, not a guarantee of control

The December 23, 2024 article introduces SAE interpretation and steering for Llama 3.3 70B. Its feature browser and API are historical: the downloaded page contains a February 2026 notice that the SAE demo interface and API were deprecated. This companion teaches the underlying method offline and does not require that service. Optional preparation: the earlier Llama SAE method and representation geometry.

What is being mapped?

Let $d_k\in\mathbb R^d$ be decoder direction $k$ of an SAE dictionary. A two-dimensional visualization assigns each direction a location $u_k\in\mathbb R^2$, using UMAP to approximately preserve selected neighborhood relationships. Nearby labels may suggest related concepts. But distances, cluster areas, and gaps in the drawing are not literal measurements of semantic volume or causal independence inside the model.

The source finds isolated features related to special formatting tokens and repeated chat elements. It offers two hypotheses: unusually large activation or feature magnitudes, and frequent memorized strings with distinctive representations. These are competing possibilities, not settled explanations. The preprocessing and distance metric matter: normalizing directions removes magnitude from the geometry, so an audit must know what vectors actually entered the map.

A map can also show distinct behavioral labels without showing distinct causal effects. The article explicitly notes that some fine-grained behavior clusters were not verified as different interventions. Browsing supplies hypotheses for controlled tests; it does not complete those tests.

Evaluate more than the desired style

For activation $h$, decoder matrix $D$, and original feature vector $z$, a residual-preserving steering intervention takes $h'=h+D(z'-z)$. If only feature $k$ changes by $s$, then $h'=h+sd_k$. For downstream scalar score $F$, first-order sensitivity is $F(h+sd_k)-F(h)\approx s\nabla_hF^\top d_k$. This approximation is local and does not guarantee a monotone behavioral effect at larger strengths.

The article's pirate-style example makes a more consequential point: style success and factual correctness can separate. At stronger intervention, the model-written style/coherence score remains high even when factual claims become incorrect. Its informal “centiClaude” score is an evaluator's rating, not a physical unit or a calibrated measure of truth.

Original numerical example: a misleading composite metric

Suppose three invented strengths yield style scores $(0.2,0.9,1.0)$ and factuality scores $(1.0,0.9,0.4)$. An evaluator attending only to style selects the strongest intervention. If the application requires factuality of at least 0.85, only the first two are eligible and the middle intervention is preferred.

A weighted average can hide the same issue. With score $U=0.9S+0.1F$, where $S$ is style and $F$ factuality, the middle case scores 0.9 and the strongest 0.94. A deployment constraint $F\ge0.85$ is not equivalent to giving factuality a small weight. State constraints before tuning, particularly when a compelling style can mask content errors.

The tradeoffs the release actually acknowledges

The source suggests narrower, sparser middle-layer SAEs work better for steering in its experience, whereas classification may benefit from broader interpreters or different layers. This is an empirical engineering observation with task dependence, not a theorem about optimal SAE width. The later practical guide gives a more skeptical recommendation for using SAEs on known supervised targets.

The article also states that SAEs capture only a fraction of model computation, due both to data coverage and reconstruction limitations. A clean feature map does not visualize what the dictionary misses. If a failure mechanism lives in the residual or in nonlinear/multidimensional structure, the map may omit it altogether.

Feature moderation removes some available steering directions; the quoted removal proportions also include dead features. They cannot be read as estimates of harmful knowledge prevalence or as directly comparable safety scores between models. Removing a direction from an exposed API does not remove the corresponding capability from the underlying language model.

A controlled follow-up would score style, factuality, coherence, and task success separately across prompts and strengths. Compare prompting, random directions, and supervised steering vectors at matched cost. Human inspection of failures remains important when the automated evaluator shares the same stylistic blind spots that steering amplifies.

Exercises

  1. Concept. Why do two neighboring labeled features require intervention tests before calling them distinct controls?
  2. Math. For the middle and strongest invented interventions, solve for weights $w$ where $wS+(1-w)F$ prefers the strongest.
  3. Critique. How would you test whether map isolation comes from magnitude or a distinctive direction?
